7.2.3.2 Camera Parameters 
The internal camera parameters include
 Cell Width (Sx) and Cell Height (Sy) in &mu; m,
 Focal Length in mm,
 Center Column (Cx) and Center Row (Cy) ,
 Image Width and Image Height in pixels. They also include
 Kappa in m &thinsp;&minus;&thinsp;2 or instead of Kappa , the distortion parameters
 Radial 2nd Order (K1) in m &thinsp;&minus;&thinsp;2 ,
 Radial 4th Order (K2) in m &thinsp;&minus;&thinsp;4 ,
 Radial 6th Order in m &thinsp;&minus;&thinsp;6 ,
 Tangential 2nd Order (P1) and
 Tangential 2nd Order (P2) in m &thinsp;&minus;&thinsp;2 
for the polynomial area scan camera model.
If you have a line scan camera, additionally to the values of the
 area scan camera (division) model, values for the motion parameters
 Motion x (Vx) , Motion y (Vy) and Motion z (Vz) in &mu; /pixel
will be returned.
